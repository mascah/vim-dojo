Cutting cold starts in half

By Priya Raman, 3 October

Our checkout service took four seconds to answer its first request after a deploy. Most of that time went into loading templates (all 312 of them) and opening a database pool that was far larger than it needed to be.

Load templates on demand

We now parse a template the first time a page needs it. The render() helper keeps a small cache keyed by path.

Size the pool for the real load

  • Measure peak connections (we saw 14, not 100)
  • Set the pool to twice that peak
  • Open connections lazily after the first request
